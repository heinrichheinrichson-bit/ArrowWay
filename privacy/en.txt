Privacy · arrow.joy

Your game data stays on your device.
No account is needed. Progress, settings and puzzles are stored locally. You can export and import your app state yourself. The app never sends this backup automatically. Android system backup is disabled. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data; exported files remain separately.

Non-personalized advertising
We use Google AdMob for occasional ads. All ad requests use child age treatment and the strictest content rating, G. Google processes technical data such as IP address, approximate region, app and ad interactions, device information, technical identifiers and diagnostics. Data is encrypted in transit. The app does not request the Android advertising ID or send game progress or backup files to AdMob. No new ads are loaded while offline.
